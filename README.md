# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis using Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** created using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The analysis focuses on understanding sales performance, profitability, category performance, regional performance, shipping costs, and key business insights.

The dashboard provides interactive filters for **Region** and **Category**, allowing users to explore the data from different perspectives.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform e-commerce sales data into meaningful business insights using Power BI.

The analysis focuses on:

* Sales performance
* Profitability
* Customer and order analysis
* Category performance
* Regional performance
* Shipping cost efficiency
* Profit drivers
* Identification of areas requiring further review

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales & Category Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales and profit across different product categories, including:

* Food
* Disposables
* Grooming
* Electronics
* Supplements
* Pet Food
* Cleaning Supplies

Food and Disposables contribute strongly to overall sales and profit performance.

---

### 2. Regional Performance

The regional analysis compares:

* Total Sales
* Profit
* Total Orders

The dashboard includes four regions:

* Central
* East
* Other
* West

The analysis shows differences in sales and profitability across regions and highlights areas requiring further review.

---

### 3. Shipping Analysis

A dedicated shipping analysis was created to understand the impact of shipping costs on profitability.

Key metrics include:

* Shipping Cost %
* Shipping Cost per Order
* Shipping Cost per Unit
* Profit After Shipping
* Total Quantity
* Shipping Cost by Category

The dashboard indicates that shipping costs can have a significant impact on profitability, particularly for categories with higher shipment volumes.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### Sales & Profit

Sales and profit are concentrated in a smaller number of categories. Food and Disposables make strong contributions to both sales and profit.

### Regional Performance

Regional performance varies considerably. The East and West regions contribute significantly to overall sales and profit, while the Other region has a relatively small contribution.

### Shipping Efficiency

Shipping costs represent an important area for profitability analysis. Categories with higher shipping costs should be reviewed to identify opportunities for improving shipping efficiency.

### Profit Drivers

Food is a major contributor to category-level profit, followed by Disposables and Grooming.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file from the **PowerBI** folder.
2. Open the file using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. If required, update the source-data path.
4. Refresh the data.
5. Use the Region and Category slicers to interact with the dashboard.
6. Explore the sales, profit, regional, category, and shipping analysis.
